Data Deletion Instructions

How to Delete Your Data

If you want to delete your Mission-X account and all associated data, you have several options:

Option 1: Remove App from Facebook

  1. Go to your Facebook Settings
  2. Click on "Apps and Websites" in the left menu
  3. Find "Mission-X" in your list of apps
  4. Click "Remove" to revoke access
  5. Check the box to "Delete all posts, photos and videos on Facebook that Mission-X may have published on your behalf"
  6. Click "Remove" to confirm

This will automatically trigger a data deletion request to our system.

Option 2: Contact Us Directly

Send an email to our support team requesting account deletion:

Email: drivephotoz330@gmail.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Include: The email address associated with your account

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When you request data deletion, we will remove:

  • Your user account information
  • Your email address and profile data
  • Your Facebook ID link
  • Any customer profile data
  • All session and authentication data

Deletion Timeline

Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of receiving your request. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

Data Retention

Some data may be retained for legal or regulatory purposes, such as:

  • Transaction records (if applicable)
  • Data required by law to be retained

This retained data will be securely stored and not used for any other purpose.
